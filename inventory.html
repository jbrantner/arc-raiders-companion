      Keys stay on this device only. They are never sent anywhere except ArcTracker.

      How to get the keys (about 10 min, once)
      1. On arctracker.io, sign in and link your ARC Raiders account so it can see your stash.
      2. Open Developers › Apps and register an app (any name, e.g. "My Companion"). Copy the app key (arc_k1_…).
      3. Open Settings › Developer Access and create a personal key. Tick stash:read, plus hideout:read, quests:read and projects:read so your plan fills in by itself. Copy it (arc_u1_…).
      4. Paste both keys above and tap Save & sync.
